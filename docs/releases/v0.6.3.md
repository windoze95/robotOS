robotOS now uses its own mark everywhere: the R1 outline from the host console, with its screen
and wheel, replaces the microphone as the app icon and on the recovery cover.

The glowing logo now stays until the R1 reaches its host, then fades straight to your screen.
Swipe it away to see the recovery screen with **Connect to a network** and **Check recovery
status**. The logo follows your finger and settles back if the swipe is short. A restricted R1
still shows its recovery screen, with the owner's return message, after a few seconds.

App update only. The hub and Hermes tools are unchanged.

Automated tests cover the logo holding through a long check, a short drag settling back, a swipe
revealing the recovery screen, a restricted R1's return message appearing without a swipe, and
snapshots of the new app icon and cover.
