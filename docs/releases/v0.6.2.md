Waking the R1 no longer flashes the recovery screen.

Within 15 minutes of a confirmed recovery check, waking the R1 or returning to robotOS opens your
screen at once and confirms with the host in the background. If the host reports the R1 lost, the
screen is taken back immediately; if it does not answer within 10 seconds, the screen is taken
back and the grace ends, so the next wake checks first. The grace lives in memory only: a reboot
or app restart always checks before showing anything.

When the screen does have to be covered, it now goes black with the robotOS logo glowing in the
center. A check that passes fades straight back to your screen, after at least a short beat so it
never flickers. The recovery page fades in only if the check takes longer than 3.5 seconds.

Recovery checks right after a reconnect finish sooner: the first retry comes after 1 second
instead of 3, and a connection attempt Tailscale drops while it is still reconnecting gives up after
3 seconds instead of 5.

Also fixed: after the recovery screen closed, the scroll wheel could stop scrolling the screen
underneath, because it was still aimed at the closed recovery page.

App update only. The hub and Hermes tools are unchanged.

Automated tests cover grace wakes that are confirmed, unanswered or reported lost, wakes after the
grace window, and the logo-then-page timing, including a short logo beat for a check that passes
at once.
