Waking the R1 out of pocket mode no longer opens your screen and then snatches it back.

Within 15 minutes of a confirmed check, a wake opens at once and the host confirms it in the
background. That confirmation used to have 10 seconds, counted from the wake. After the 3-minute
radio idle cut, though, the radios take a few seconds to return, and Tailscale can then need
WireGuard's 15-second re-handshake before any traffic flows. A normal wake from a pocket could miss
the window, and the glowing logo would cover the screen you were already using (#48).

- The host now gets 20 seconds from the moment a usable network is back, and never more than 30
  seconds after the wake, so an R1 that is kept offline still locks.
- A lost or revoked reply still takes the screen back immediately.
- While a grace wake waits for its confirmation, the R1 retries about every 2 seconds instead of
  backing off.
- A wake switches the radios back on once. The button press, screen-on and recovery check could
  each send the root "airplane off, Wi-Fi on" command before the first one finished, so the modem
  saw a second airplane-mode change while it was reconnecting (#50).
- Each recovery check now logs its outcome and duration (the kind of failure only, never the host's
  address), which makes slow wakes diagnosable from the R1 itself.

App update only. The hub and Hermes tools are unchanged.

Automated tests cover a pocket wake whose network returns after the old window, a grace wake that
never gets a network and is recaptured at the cap, and a single radio command for back-to-back wake
triggers. On a physical R1 running a signed 0.6.4 preflight build, a wake after the idle cut opened
at once, sent one radio command and was confirmed 2.8 seconds later. With no network at all, the
grace wake was recaptured 30.0 seconds after the wake, and it was verified about 3 seconds after the
radios were switched back on.
