Texting Hermes can now require a passphrase, so a text that fakes your number can't reach Hermes.

- **Passphrase for your texts.** Set one under **Hermes → Your texts to Hermes**: 6 to 64
  characters. Start each text to Hermes with it, for example `otter-42 what's on today?`. Case is
  ignored, and a space, colon or comma must follow it. Hermes gets the text without the passphrase,
  and doesn't see it when it reads your texts with `recent_texts`. Texts from your number without
  it stay in Messages. Without a passphrase, the relay works as before, and the screen explains
  what that risks.
- **Guessing is limited.** Five texts from your number without the passphrase within an hour pause
  the relay until fewer than five fall within the past hour. The Hermes screen and the on-device
  event log show when that happens.
- **A change takes effect at once.** Changing or clearing the passphrase cancels owner texts still
  waiting for Hermes or for their reply.

App update only. The hub and Hermes tools are unchanged. On a direct connection the passphrase can
also be set with `hermes.passphrase`.

Automated tests cover the following:
- texts without the passphrase staying in Messages, and Hermes getting the text without it;
- the pause after repeated texts without it, and the relay resuming after an hour;
- a passphrase change cancelling queued replies;
- `recent_texts` leaving the passphrase out of the owner's texts;
- saving, importing and validating the passphrase.
