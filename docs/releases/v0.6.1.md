Fixes a bug that could leave a paired R1 locked on the recovery screen after waking away from
home, and the overnight battery drain behind it.

With Tailscale connected, robotOS treated the VPN as a working connection even when no network was
under it. With the screen off, the 3-minute radio idle cut and the recovery check undid each other:
each cut looked like a network change, the check switched the radios back on, and the cut re-armed.
Airplane mode toggled about every 3 minutes all night. One of those quick toggles could leave mobile
data stuck "connecting". On the next wake the R1 showed cellular signal but had no data, so the
recovery check could not reach the host until Android dropped the stuck connection minutes later.

- With the screen off, a recovery check never switches the radios back on.
- A network counts as online only when Wi-Fi, mobile data or another real link is under the VPN.
  Voice turns, dictation and Hermes texts wait for the radios to reconnect after an idle cut instead
  of sending into a dead tunnel.
- Under Tailscale, a real reconnect triggers one recovery check; bandwidth updates no longer do.
- The recovery screen retries on its own while it is showing (after 3 seconds, backing off to every
  30 seconds) and says whether the R1 has no internet connection or the host has not answered.
  **Stop ringing** appears only when the R1 is restricted or ringing. The screen no longer redraws
  continuously while it is up.

App update only. The hub and Hermes tools are unchanged.

Automated tests cover the pocket-mode cut under a VPN, VPN-without-network detection, reconnect
checks and the recovery screen's layout behavior. On a physical R1 running a signed 0.6.1 preflight
build on battery with Tailscale over mobile data, the idle cut held airplane mode on for two minutes
without bouncing. On wake, mobile data returned in 2.0 seconds and recovery was verified in
2.5 seconds.
